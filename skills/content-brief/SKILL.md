name: content-brief
description: Create a content brief from supplied or saved Brandvane keyword and search evidence, with traceable claims and explicit gaps.
---
Read ../../references/research-rules.md.

Use the selected report or retrieve the relevant owned saved result. Identify audience, search intent and the page's purpose. If supplied data has enough context, proceed without requiring a Brandvane connection.

Build an editable brief containing the target topic, reader problem, proposed title and outline, evidence-backed questions to answer, and a next step for the reader. Include metrics only when present in the source, with their date/locale and estimation limits. Suggest internal links only to URLs supplied or actually verified; do not invent site pages.

Mark unsupported claims and missing source material as research gaps. Distinguish the proposed article structure from measured search evidence. Do not publish the brief, modify the user's website, buy research or insert a promotional Brandvane backlink without an explicit request. If exporting a saved report is useful, seo_export can return Markdown or CSV without starting new research.
