Read ../../references/research-rules.md.

Establish the target domain, audience and market from the request or evidence. Ask only for missing facts that change the recommendations. If a report is supplied, analyze it directly without requiring an account.

For connected research, use seo_usage and seo_result to find relevant completed evidence. Select only jobs in the user's chosen workspace. If fresh evidence is needed, propose the smallest useful quote: keyword_research for ideas, competing_keywords for one competitor, keyword_gap for a two-domain comparison, or referring_domains for backlink sources. Do not treat competitor keyword lists as a measured two-domain gap.

Return a short prioritized plan with the supporting observation, recommended action and a way to verify it. Separate estimated demand from proven conversions. Do not convert a referring-domain result into a promise that those sites will link to the user. If evidence is too sparse, say which decision remains uncertain rather than filling the gap with invented metrics.
